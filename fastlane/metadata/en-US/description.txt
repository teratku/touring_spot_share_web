Record the roads you've ridden, discover and share your favorite spots, and build up your riding achievements — an all-in-one app for motorcycle touring enthusiasts.

■ GPS Route Recording
・Real-time GPS route recording with background support
・Automatic distance, duration, and road name tracking
・Elevation data and per-road segment editing
・Attach photos to spots along your route

■ Discover & Post Spots
・Search scenic spots and rest stops on map or list
・Post spots with photos to share with other riders
・Reviews and star ratings from fellow riders

■ Touring Plans
・Build touring plans by combining spots
・"Random suggestion" mode when you can't decide where to go
・View all your plans on a map at a glance

■ Achievements & Gamification
・Earn badges for distance ridden, number of routes, spots posted, and more
・Visualize prefecture conquest across all 47 prefectures of Japan
・Stamp rallies to make exploring new spots more fun
・Road completion map showing your coverage grid by grid

■ Route Sharing
・Publish and share your recorded routes with other users
・Privacy-aware sharing controls

Whether you're riding with friends or solo, the more you ride, the more you record, discover, and achieve.
