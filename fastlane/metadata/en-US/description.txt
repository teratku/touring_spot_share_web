Enter a destination and get a route along twisty, scenic "recommended roads", with spoken directions before every turn. Every ride is mapped, and the places you find become another rider's next destination.
Discover, ride and record — one touring app built for motorcycles.

■ Discover
・Explore views, twisty roads, roadside stations (michi-no-eki), hot springs and food spots posted by riders across Japan, on a map or in a list
・Each spot shows highlights such as Scenic view or Easy bike parking, plus fun roads nearby
・Find mountain passes and coastal roads by curviness, length and tags (Scenic, Free-flowing, Gravel-ish)
・Popularity rankings of roads and spots riders picked, plus "For you" suggestions based on your taste
・Can't decide where to go? Pick a radius and direction for a random suggestion
・Check winter closure dates of skylines and passes, with "closes in N days"
・Favorite the roads you love and prefer them when building routes

■ Ride (navigation built for riders)
・Routes that pick and link fun roads while keeping detours small
・Feel like the long way? "Go Wide" mode — and Boost to reach passes even farther away
・Don't like a road on the route? Remove it and rebuild, one road at a time
・Avoids roads your bike can't ride (50cc, 125cc or larger) and warns at intersections that need a two-step right turn
・Choose toll roads and expressways for each stop
・Set an arrival time and we'll tell you when to leave, with the weather when you arrive and an easy add to your calendar
・Set a break interval and a well-placed roadside station is added as a stop
・Spoken directions before every turn, including expressway junctions and exits, lane hints, and the start and end of recommended roads
・Distance to the next service area, and roadside stations ahead you can add as a stop on the spot
・Automatic rerouting, and the next turn on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

■ Record
・GPS ride recording in the background, with distance, time, road names and elevation
・Recommended roads are marked as ridden automatically from your rides — see which passes are still left
・Post a spot just by picking a photo; its location comes from the photo. Add highlights so riders can find it
・Prefecture conquest map, badges, stamp rallies and road completion — the more you ride, the more you collect
・Share recorded routes (the area around your start is trimmed automatically) and back them up to the cloud (Plus)

■ On the web too
・Create and edit touring plans, browse recommended roads and winter closures from your computer (biketeilen.web.app)

[Pricing]
Navigation, route building, recording and posting are free, with no usage limits.
Plus adds unlimited Go Wide mode (free: 3 per month), roadside stations ahead during navigation, 10 stops per plan (free: 3), all recommended roads in lists, cloud backup of your rides, route building on the web, no ads, and more.

[Notes]
